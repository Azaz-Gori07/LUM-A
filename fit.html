THE FIT GUIDE

Asked, answered.

Level, all day. If it rides up at the back, the band is too big — size down and cup up. The band carries the support; the straps are only steering.

Spill at the edge — take the fuller cup. Gap at the top — take the smaller. Genuinely between both? Write to the fit team; a human answers within a day.

Two fingers slide under the band; three don’t. It should feel like a handshake, not a grip.

Take the smaller band. Memory knit relaxes to you over about two weeks — never the other way around — so snug-then-perfect beats perfect-then-loose.
