04 — THE ATELIER

Nine weeks, thirty-four hands.

LUMÉA runs are cut, bonded and finished across two ateliers — Calais for the lace, Como for the knits. Nothing leaves until it survives a forty-point hand inspection. This is what slow actually costs, and why we pay it.

THE MAKING — A RUN, WEEK BY WEEK

W01

The sketch

One pencil, one block, forty fittings — until the line stops arguing with the body and starts agreeing with it.

W02

The block

Pattern geometry is frozen for the run. From here to W09, not a millimeter moves without three people signing it.

W03

Como mills the knit

Memory knit is loomed to spec — density mapped panel by panel, then rested 48 hours before anyone touches it.

W05

Bonding trials

Heat, pressure, dwell. Eleven attempts before the seam fuses flat and stays flat. Attempt twelve becomes the bra.

W07

The forty points

Every single piece, by hand, under 3× magnification. A quarter of the run never makes it past this week.

W09

The fold & wrap

Tissue, wax seal, batch number in pencil. Then — and only then — it becomes something you can forget you’re wearing.
